Executive Findings

Two active strategy chains:
Race Captain top cards use generatePredictiveStrategy(); native energy command cards use buildRacePrediction() -> planBatterySwap() -> buildDeterministicStrategyRecommendation().
Most trust is Medium:
Most displayed strategy numbers are forecasts using live telemetry plus route/config fallbacks. Direct telemetry fields are High only when ESP32/cloud packets are fresh.
Stage 2 watch item:
Solar recovery and stopped/trailering recovery now exist in the prediction chain, but similar labels in older panels can use different energy-model assumptions.

Special Calculation Chains

Recommended Speed
Top cards: telemetry/route/car setup -> simulateDayEnergy() -> generatePredictiveStrategy() -> recommendSpeed(). Native cards: telemetry/history/route -> buildRacePrediction() -> planBatterySwap() -> buildDeterministicStrategyRecommendation().
Medium
Race Health
Telemetry source/status + predictive strategy -> calculateRaceHealthBreakdown() -> score and label.
Medium
Mission Status
Predictive strategy -> swap debug next-opportunity SOC + trailering action + highest route risk -> classifyMissionStatus().
Medium
Strategy Recommendation
Telemetry/history/route -> prediction -> swap planner -> battery state validation -> deterministic recommendation command, reason, speed, warnings.
Medium
Projected End-Day SOC
Telemetry battery energy or SOC estimate -> schedule events -> drive energy minus MPPT recovery -> projectedEndDaySocPercent.
Medium
Projected Next Stop SOC
Same prediction path, but projected to next schedule event or route stop rather than full day.
Medium
Predicted Wh/mi
Rolling recent Wh/mi -> live Wh/mi -> fallback 40 Wh/mi -> clamp 25-75 Wh/mi.
Medium
Solar Recovery
MPPT priority: charge power -> MPPT power -> solar power -> station fallback. Schedule applies recovery during driving, stops, and trailering.
Medium
Swap Recommendation
Active/spare pack SOC -> prediction SOC projections -> force swap 20%, plan swap 30%, spare advantage 10% -> action.
Medium
Trailering Recommendation
15-mile route lookahead + risk/type multipliers + current SOC + baseline Wh/mi -> projected driven/trailered SOC -> action.

Suspicious or Inconsistent Metrics

  • Race Captain top cards and native command cards can show different recommended speeds because they use different strategy chains.
  • Race Health and Mission Status use the older predictive strategy swap advisor, while native Battery Strategy uses the newer race battery planner.
  • There are two active swap planners: batterySwapAdvisor.ts and raceBatteryStrategy.ts.
  • Solar recovery appears in several places but can be sourced from schedule forecast, integrated history, MPPT daily energy, or simulation depending on component.
  • Simulator climb current can still reach 120A, which may produce harsher simulated power than the new Wh/mi targets suggest.
  • Descent simulator efficiency is hardcoded at 24 Wh/mi, close to the lower realism floor.

Verify Before Race Testing

  • Pick one authoritative strategy chain for Race Captain decisions.
  • Confirm live ESP32/cloud telemetry includes reliable batteryEnergyWh; otherwise predictions estimate from SOC.
  • Validate MPPT priority and units: mpptChargePowerWatts, mpptPowerWatts, solarPowerWatts.
  • Compare Projected Next Stop SOC across prediction summary, next event, swap advisor, and debug panel.
  • Review schedule stop/trailer durations because they directly drive solar recovery and end-day SOC.
  • Recheck simulator current/power behavior after calibration, especially climb and descent cases.
